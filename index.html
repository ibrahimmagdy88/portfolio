The journey · 2020 – 2026

From graphic designer to product designer

The pixel

I studied commerce at Cairo University, but design found me during military service. I made the unit’s banners and visuals, then designed my first interface in Adobe XD: a small system for officers’ leave requests. After service I joined NTI’s graphic design program, took my first freelance UI project with a developer (a book-selling and reading-meetups platform) and interned at Nahdet Misr.

  • Military service · Jan 2020 – Mar 2021
  • NTI Graphic Design · Jun – Jul 2021
  • Nahdet Misr internship · Oct 2021
January 2022400%

The screen

I joined Fantastic Hosting as a graphic designer after passing a brand-identity test. On day one, the task was a complete clinic-booking app, Tabibak, and I was the only designer in the company. I learned fast and shipped it. Looking back, I skipped research and user flows, had no design system or components, and even put hover states on mobile buttons. Those mistakes became my checklist.

  • Tabibak, my first app
  • Al Braheem: 8 connected products
Mid – late 2022200%

The experience

Good-looking screens weren’t enough, so I went deeper: advanced prototyping, then Udacity’s Advanced UX Nanodegree, where I learned interviews, surveys, research synthesis and reporting. On Adelco I ran my first structured process: stakeholder sessions, competitor analysis, information architecture, wireframes and a style guide, on a phased delivery plan. In October I redesigned Tabibak and could see how far I’d come.

  • Udacity Advanced UX · 3 months
  • Adelco · Al Khwarizmi · Tabibak v2
2023 – 2024100%

The system

Most of my work became multi-sided systems: one product for customers, others for drivers, technicians, merchants, banks or school staff, all sharing one backbone. iGold combined commerce, POS and inventory. Highway grew new services after launch. Tasahl ran on my first design system with light and dark modes. In parallel I designed fintech and marketplace products for Cashpoint, and in 2024 took Syntax’s Advanced UX/UI program.

  • Cashpoint, part-time · 2022 – 2024
  • Syntax Advanced UX/UI · 2024
2025 – nowFit

The product

At Techeffic I work on live travel products that people search and book on every day. Design is one part of the product cycle: I frame problems with product managers, decide with data, review builds with engineering and QA, maintain a design system shared by several products, and use AI tools to research and explore faster.

  • Techeffic · Senior UI/UX Designer
  • Flights · hotels · transfers · sports

In 2022 I was learning to design a screen. Then an experience. Then a whole system. Now I help build products that keep evolving.
